01 — THE STORY

Not one thing.
Not just one thing.

I have always found it difficult to put life into neat little boxes. Maybe that's a good thing.

I believe in fantasy, but I respect practicality. I am curious about almost everything, even while knowing that one lifetime is nowhere near enough time to learn it all.

Cinema has always been more than entertainment to me. It is a language, a feeling, a place I keep returning to. I may be doing a white-collar job right now, but somewhere in my heart, the destination is still a film set.

Still figuring it out. Happily.
